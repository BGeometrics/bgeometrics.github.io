Ethereum NUPL (Net Unrealized Profit/Loss)

Share of the market capitalization of Ethereum that is unrealized profit or loss: what ETH holders would gain or lose if they all sold now.

NUPL = (Market Cap - Realized Cap) / Market Cap

Values close to 0 or negative point to capitulation and fear; high values point to euphoria, when most of the market is in profit and selling pressure can increase.

Calculated by BGeometrics from every address balance change since genesis, valuing each address at its average acquisition cost; ETH sent to the staking deposit contract keeps its cost and is not counted as a sale. Derived from ethpandaops xatu public data (CC BY 4.0).
