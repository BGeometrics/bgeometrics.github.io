Ethereum MVRV (Market Value to Realized Value)

Ratio between the market capitalization of Ethereum and its realized capitalization, the sum of what every ETH in circulation cost to acquire.

MVRV = Market Cap / Realized Cap

Above 1, ETH holders are in profit on average; below 1, in loss. Historically, high values have marked periods of overvaluation and profit-taking, and values below 1 have marked periods of undervaluation and accumulation.

Calculated by BGeometrics from every address balance change since genesis, valuing each address at its average acquisition cost; ETH sent to the staking deposit contract keeps its cost and is not counted as a sale. Derived from ethpandaops xatu public data (CC BY 4.0).
