Bitcoin Options Volatility

UpVol and DownVol measure realized volatility separately for upside and downside moves in Bitcoin options markets; Skew Index is the difference between them (UpVol minus DownVol).

A positive skew means upside moves have been more volatile than downside moves recently — the market has been "melting up". A negative skew means downside moves have dominated — fear is elevated relative to greed.
