Bitcoin NRPL (Net Realized Profit Loss)

This metric is used to measure the net profit or loss of all bitcoin transactions based on the price at which they were bought and sold.
This metric gives insight into overall investor sentiment and behavior by indicating whether bitcoin holders are making a profit or a loss.

The interpretation of net realized profit/loss
- Positive NRPL: Indicates that most market participants are selling their Bitcoins at a profit.
- Negative NRPL: Suggests that most market participants are selling their Bitcoins at a loss.
- Sharp increases or decreases: Large swings in the NRPL metric can signify potential reversals or continuations in the market trend as they can indicate significant changes in market sentiment.

Investors often use the NRPL metric to:
- Identify potential lows and highs in the market.
- Understand overall market sentiment (is the market overall in profit or loss?)
- Anticipate potential market corrections or rallies based on profit-taking or loss-cutting behavior.

NRPL USD
It measures the net dollar value of profit or loss realized by bitcoin holders over a given period.

NRPL BTC
The same net profit or loss expressed in BTC: the realized profit minus the realized loss, valued at the price of each day. It measures the size of the profit or loss, not the amount of bitcoin sold.

NRPL LTH and NRPL STH
The NRPL split by type of holder: long-term holders (LTH, coins held for more than 155 days) and short-term holders (STH). Each one is the realized profit minus the realized loss of that group, in BTC, and together they add up to NRPL BTC.
• NRPL LTH high and positive: experienced holders taking profits, typical of the later stages of bull markets. Negative: long-term holders selling at a loss, which is rare and usually happens near cycle bottoms.
• NRPL STH swings with every price move, because recent buyers react quickly: persistent losses often mark panic selling after a drop, and persistent profits euphoria after a rally.
• Comparing both lines shows who is behind the profits or losses of the whole market: recent buyers or long-term holders.

NRPL LTH 7D MA and NRPL STH 7D MA
7-day moving averages of NRPL LTH and NRPL STH. They smooth out the daily noise and make it easier to see when a group changes from realizing profits to realizing losses, or the other way round. These four lines are hidden by default: click their names in the legend to show them, or see them together with profit and loss by group in Realized Profit Loss LTH STH.

NRPL LTH USD and NRPL STH USD
The net realized profit or loss of each group in US dollars: how many dollars long-term and short-term holders gained (positive) or lost (negative) on the coins they moved that day. Together they add up to NRPL USD, so they show which group is behind each day's gains or losses. Because they are in dollars, the same amount of BTC weighs more at high prices: they are best compared with each other, or with NRPL USD, on the same day. These two lines are also hidden by default.
