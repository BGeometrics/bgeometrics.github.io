Ethereum ETF Flows USD

Daily net flow of all U.S. spot Ethereum ETFs, in USD. Weekends and holidays show 0.

Positive flows mean new money buying ETH through the ETFs; negative flows mean outflows. Sustained inflows add structural demand for ETH; sustained outflows remove it.

Updated daily, with a delay of about 2 days.
