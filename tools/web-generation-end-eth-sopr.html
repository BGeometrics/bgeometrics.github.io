Ethereum SOPR (Spent Output Profit Ratio)

Ratio between the value of the ETH spent each day and what that ETH cost when it was acquired.

SOPR = Value of ETH spent / Cost of ETH spent

Above 1, the ETH moved that day was sold at a profit on average; below 1, at a loss. In bull markets SOPR tends to stay above 1 and dips to 1 act as support, because holders stop selling at break-even; in bear markets the level of 1 acts as resistance.

Calculated by BGeometrics from every address balance change since genesis, valuing each address at its average acquisition cost; ETH sent to the staking deposit contract keeps its cost and is not counted as a sale. Derived from ethpandaops xatu public data (CC BY 4.0).
