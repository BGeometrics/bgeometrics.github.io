Bitcoin Miner Reserve 30-Day Change (%)

Percentage change of the miner reserve over the last 30 days. Sustained negative values (red) mark periods in which miners are net sellers.

Calculated by BGeometrics from our own Bitcoin full node. Miner addresses are the coinbase payout addresses of the mining pools plus the wallets that receive their funds, identified and extended every day. Inflow is the BTC received by those addresses (including the block reward); outflow is the BTC spent from them.

Address attribution never covers every miner wallet, so absolute levels are an estimate. Data before 4 November 2023 comes from an earlier address list and its flow levels are not comparable with the current series.
