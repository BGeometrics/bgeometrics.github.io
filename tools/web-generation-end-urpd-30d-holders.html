Realized Price Distribution: Supply Moved in the Last 30 Days by STH and LTH

The realized price distribution of UTXO (URPD) is a bar chart where at each purchase price it is indicated how much volume of BTC has been purchased. In this version each $1,000 bar shows the BTC that last moved more than 30 days ago and, on top of it, the BTC moved in the last 30 days split by who moved it: short-term holders (STH) or long-term holders (LTH).

Each UTXO created in the last 30 days is assigned to LTH or STH according to the coins its transaction spent: the share of spent value that was 155 days old or more counts as LTH, the rest as STH. Newly mined coins count as STH. LTH bars at recent prices show at what price levels long-term holders have been distributing.

Only the last hop is visible: if an LTH sends coins to an exchange and the exchange then moves them between its own wallets, that second move counts as STH. The LTH part is therefore a lower bound of LTH distribution.

Recently moved supply shows where the market has been re-pricing coins: a large highlighted block near the current price means many coins changed hands there recently, typical of short-term holders, while price levels that only hold old supply reflect longer-term conviction.

When there are high bars together in a price range, it usually indicates a support zone for that price since a good amount of bitcoin was purchased at those prices.

When there are low bars in a price range, it indicates that if the market price reaches that area there may be volatility since there are few investors who have bought at those prices.
