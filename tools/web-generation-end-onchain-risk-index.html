Bitcoin On-Chain Risk Index

The On-Chain Risk Index is a composite market risk indicator (0-100) combining three equal-weight components: the Macro Risk Index (MVRV + Mayer Multiple long-cycle composite), the STH Risk Index (SOPR STH + Loss Ratio + MVRV STH short-cycle composite), and an Options Skew Risk Score (normalized percentile of UpVol minus DownVol from Deribit options; high positive skew means calls are expensive, i.e. bullish sentiment, which reads as elevated risk).

Each component is normalized by its own cumulative historical percentile rank, so the composite has no look-ahead bias. Higher values indicate historically elevated risk; lower values indicate accumulation zones.

The line color follows the score itself, matching the five documented risk zones: Accumulation (0-20), Low Risk (20-40), Moderate (40-60), Elevated (60-80) and High Risk (80-100).

The index is only available for dates where options data (upvol, downvol) is present, so its history is shorter than most other on-chain metrics on this site.
