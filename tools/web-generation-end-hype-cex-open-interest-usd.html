Hyperliquid (HYPE) CEX Open Interest

Open interest of the HYPE perpetual futures on Binance, Bybit and OKX at the daily close, in USD: the value of all open positions.

The series starts on 15 October 2026 because Binance only publishes the last 30 days of open interest; it grows by one day every day.

Rising open interest means new money and leverage entering the market. Rising open interest with a rising price suggests new longs; with a falling price, new shorts. A sharp drop in open interest usually follows liquidations or positions being closed.
