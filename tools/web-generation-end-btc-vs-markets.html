Bitcoin vs S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and Gold

This chart compares Bitcoin with the main traditional assets: US equities (S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100) and gold. Because their prices are on very different scales, every line shows the percentage change from the first day of the selected range. Change the range with the buttons or the navigator and all lines are rebased to that new start.

The boxes under the chart show each asset's return over the visible range and its last price. Hover the chart to see the price and the change of every asset on a given day. Click an asset in the legend to hide or show it.

Bitcoin uses daily closes, 4-hour candles for the last year and 1-hour candles for the last week. Equities and gold use daily closes, so they only move on trading days.

Sources: S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 through the SPY and QQQ ETFs, gold is XAU/USD spot, Bitcoin price from BGeometrics.
