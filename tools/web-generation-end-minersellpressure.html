Bitcoin Miner Sell Pressure

This indicator shows when Bitcoin miners are selling more BTC than usual.
It indicates how much bitcoin miners are selling in the last 30 days in relation to the reserves they have.

It is an indicator proposed by Capriole: the miner outflows of the last 30 days are added up and divided by the average miner reserve of the last 30 days.

The graph shows the positive and negative standard deviation of this metric for the last 200 days and the relevant information is the intersection with these lines.
When it is high, it indicates more sales than usual in relation to the reservations they have.
When it is low, it indicates fewer sales than usual in relation to their reservations.

Bitcoin miner sell pressure represents industry stress, excess, and miner capitulation.

Data source and history. Miner outflows and reserves are calculated by BGeometrics from its own Bitcoin node, by tracking coinbase outputs and known mining pool addresses. The chart starts on 2026-01-18, when the set of miner addresses and the method were updated: earlier values are not comparable and are not shown. The first 30-day value is from 2026-02-16 and the 200-day bands start in September 2026.

API. The API field minerSellPressure (endpoint miner-sell-pressure) is the daily value: that day's minerOutflowBtc divided by that day's minerReserveBtc. The 30-day value shown in this chart is the 30-day sum of minerOutflowBtc divided by the 30-day average of minerReserveBtc, using data from 2026-01-18 onwards.
