Ethereum Supply

Circulating supply of Ethereum, in ETH.

Unlike Bitcoin, Ethereum has no fixed maximum supply: new ETH is issued to validators and part of the transaction fees is burned. When more ETH is burned than issued, the supply falls.

The supply trend shows whether Ethereum is inflationary or deflationary in each period.

Calculated by BGeometrics from every address balance change since genesis, valuing each address at its average acquisition cost; ETH sent to the staking deposit contract keeps its cost and is not counted as a sale. Derived from ethpandaops xatu public data (CC BY 4.0).
