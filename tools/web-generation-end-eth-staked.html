Ethereum Staked

ETH held by the staking deposit contract, net of withdrawals, from 3 November 2020.

Staked ETH secures the network and earns rewards for validators. Since withdrawals were enabled in April 2023, staked ETH can leave the contract again.

A growing amount of staked ETH takes supply out of the liquid market; a falling amount can mean more ETH available to sell.

Calculated by BGeometrics from every address balance change since genesis, valuing each address at its average acquisition cost; ETH sent to the staking deposit contract keeps its cost and is not counted as a sale. Derived from ethpandaops xatu public data (CC BY 4.0).
