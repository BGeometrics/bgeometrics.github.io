Hyperliquid (HYPE) Market Cap

Market capitalization of HYPE, the native token of the Hyperliquid blockchain, in USD.

It is calculated as the circulating supply multiplied by the daily close of HYPE. The circulating supply of the last two days is provisional and is revised when the final figure is published.

The background line is the HYPE price. Market cap and price move together; the gap between them changes as tokens enter or leave circulation through unlocks, emissions or buybacks.

To see how much of the token is still to come into circulation, compare it with the fully diluted value in the Market Cap / FDV chart.
