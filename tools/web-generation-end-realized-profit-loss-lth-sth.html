Bitcoin Realized Profit Loss LTH STH

Profit and loss realized each day by long-term holders (LTH, coins held for more than 155 days) and short-term holders (STH), expressed in BTC.

Every time coins move on-chain, the price at which they move is compared with the price at which they were acquired. If it is higher, the difference is a realized profit; if it is lower, a realized loss. The chart adds up those profits and losses for each group of holders, valued in BTC at the price of the day.

LTH Profit / LTH Loss and STH Profit / STH Loss show both sides separately: how much profit and how much loss each group took that day. Losses are shown as negative values.

NRPL LTH and NRPL STH (Net Realized Profit/Loss) show the net result of each group: its realized profit plus its realized loss.
NRPL LTH = LTH Profit + LTH Loss  ·  NRPL STH = STH Profit + STH Loss
Above 0, that group sold more coins at a profit than at a loss; below 0, the reverse. The 7D MA lines are the 7-day moving averages of each NRPL and smooth out the daily noise to show the trend.

How to read it.
• Long-term holders taking profits (high NRPL LTH) is typical of the later stages of bull markets, when experienced holders distribute coins to new buyers.
• Long-term holders realizing losses (negative NRPL LTH) is rare and usually happens in deep bear markets and capitulations, close to cycle bottoms.
• Short-term holders react faster to price: their NRPL swings between profit and loss with every move. Persistent losses by short-term holders often mark panic selling after a drop; persistent profits, euphoria after a rally.
• Comparing both groups shows who is driving the market: whether the selling comes from recent buyers or from long-term holders.

Note: values in BTC measure the size of the profit or loss, not the amount of BTC sold. For example, selling 10,000 BTC with a 10% profit realizes about 900 BTC of profit, while selling 1,000 BTC with a 100% profit realizes about 500. The data is calculated by BGeometrics from the Bitcoin blockchain since January 2015; earlier values come from an external source.
