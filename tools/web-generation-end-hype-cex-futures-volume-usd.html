Hyperliquid (HYPE) CEX Futures Volume

Daily trading volume of the HYPE perpetual futures on the centralized exchanges Binance, Bybit and OKX, in USD.

Each exchange counts from the day it listed HYPE: Bybit from December 2024, OKX from February 2025 and Binance from May 2025, so the jumps on those dates are real growth in available markets.

Volume measures interest and liquidity. Rising volume with rising prices supports the trend; very high volume with weak prices can signal distribution. It complements the volume on Hyperliquid itself, the largest market for HYPE perpetuals.
