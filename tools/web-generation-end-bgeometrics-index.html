BGeometrics Index

The BGeometrics Index combines three proprietary 0-100 gauges into a single chart: the Demand Index (buy-side pressure from spot/derivatives flow), the Short-Term Trends Index (momentum over shorter timeframes) and the Market Peaks Index (how close current conditions are to historical cycle tops).

Unlike the STH Risk Index or Cycle Extreme, which reduce several inputs into one composite score, these three gauges are shown separately so you can see when they agree (all high or all low) versus when they diverge (e.g. strong demand but low peak risk).

Each line can also be explored on its own dedicated dashboard: Demand, Short-Term Trends and Market Peaks.
